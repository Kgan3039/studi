# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** June 28, 2026

## Introduction

Studi ("we," "our," or "us") operates the Studi mobile application ("Studi" or the "App").

Studi is a student-focused platform designed to help university students discover study locations, create and join study sessions, connect with other students, and communicate through in-app messaging.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect information when you use Studi.

By using Studi, you acknowledge that your information will be handled as described in this Privacy Policy.

---

# Information We Collect

## Account Information

To use Studi, users create and maintain an account. We collect information necessary to create, authenticate, and manage accounts, including account moderation or restriction if our Terms of Service are violated.

## University Authentication

Studi currently requires users to register using a valid university email address. We use Firebase Authentication to verify account ownership and help maintain a student-only community.

## Profile Information

We may collect profile information that you choose to provide, including:

- Name
- University email address
- Academic information (such as classes or major)
- Profile photo
- Biography
- Interests
- Other profile information you choose to share within the Studi community

## Communications Information

Studi may collect and store:

- Direct messages
- User reports
- Block actions
- Other interactions between users

This information helps us provide messaging features, enforce community standards, investigate abuse, and maintain platform safety.

## Study Session Information

We collect information related to study sessions created or joined within the app, including session details, participants, locations, and related activity.

## Location and Map Data

Studi uses campus study location and map data to help users discover study spots and study sessions.

Studi does **not** currently collect or store precise real-time device location in the background. If location-based features are added in the future, users will be asked for permission through their device settings.

## Information Collected Automatically

When you use Studi, we may automatically collect certain technical information, including:

- Device type and model
- Operating system information
- App version
- Usage and interaction data
- Diagnostic and crash information

---

# How We Use Information

We use information collected through Studi to:

- Provide and maintain the App
- Create and manage user accounts
- Authenticate users
- Enable core app functionality
- Support messaging and study session features
- Improve app performance and reliability
- Enforce our Terms of Service and community safety standards

---

# Third-Party Services

We use third-party service providers to help operate Studi. These providers may access and process information only as necessary to provide services on our behalf and in a manner consistent with this Privacy Policy.

## Firebase

Studi uses Firebase services provided by Google, including:

- Firebase Authentication
- Cloud Firestore
- Cloud Functions
- Related Firebase infrastructure

These services help support account management, data storage, messaging, application functionality, and security.

Firebase may collect and process certain information as described in Google's privacy documentation.

---

# Sharing of Information

We do **not** sell personal information.

We only share information in the following circumstances:

- With service providers necessary to operate Studi
- When required by law or legal process
- To investigate fraud, abuse, or violations of our policies
- To protect the safety, security, and integrity of Studi and its users

---

# Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to:

- Provide and operate Studi
- Maintain account functionality
- Protect the security and integrity of the App
- Enforce our policies and safety requirements

Information is deleted when it is no longer needed for these purposes, unless retention is required for legal, security, fraud-prevention, or operational reasons.

---

# Account Deletion

Users may request account deletion by contacting us using the contact information below.

For security purposes, account deletion requests may require identity verification or re-authentication.

Upon account deletion, we will delete associated personal information within a reasonable timeframe, except where retention is required for legal, security, fraud-prevention, or operational purposes.

---

# Information Visible to Other Users

Certain information may be visible to other Studi users as part of normal app functionality, including:

- Profile information you choose to share
- Study sessions you create or join
- Location ratings and related activity

Direct messages are private and are only visible to participants in a conversation, except where access is necessary for moderation, safety, abuse prevention, or legal compliance.

Users should avoid sharing sensitive personal information through profiles or messages.
